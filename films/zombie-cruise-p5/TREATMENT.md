# Zombie Apocalypse on a Cruise Ship · Part 5 — treatment

**Style:** Chunky 3D Cartoon (the user's own style). 1080×1920, 24 fps, ~55 s. Same header, badge "PART 5". Narrator Kokoro `am_michael`, speed 1.05. You, Marco the deckhand and the cat in its crew vest, plus one new character: the lighthouse keeper.
**Picks up from Part 4:** the lifeboat has glided into a harbour where every light is out, and the cat looked back at us.

## What's in the dark harbour
The town isn't dead. The survivors switched every light off themselves, because light draws the zombies in. The few zombies left in town stand asleep on their feet along the quay. The survivors hide in the lighthouse and leave chalk arrows for anyone still alive. Then comes the sting: you left the cruise ship's engines running, and the ship, full of zombies, is heading for the harbour.

## Candidate structures
1. **Ghost town walk.** Wander the empty streets and find nothing until the very end. It's moody, but the rules stay abstract and the middle sags.
2. **Quiet in, grin, sting (chosen).** Slip past sleeping zombies, follow the chalk arrows, then grin for the torch at the lighthouse door. You find people at last, and the relief turns when the ship appears out of the fog. Every rule gets a physical gag (the engine lever, the smiley arrow, the big grin), and the ending pays off Part 3 (the engines) and Part 2 (the horn).
3. **Survivors find you.** Torches surround the boat the moment it lands. It's dramatic, but it gives the story away in the first ten seconds.

**Logline:** In a harbour where every light is out, zombies doze standing up along the quay. You cut the engine, slip past them, and follow chalk arrows with smiley faces to the lighthouse. At the lighthouse door a torch checks your faces, and your biggest grins get you in. Inside, the survivors keep the lights off on purpose. Then a horn sounds in the fog: your cruise ship, engines still running, is coming in, and its decks are packed with zombies.
