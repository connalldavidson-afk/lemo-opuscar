## Candidate structures
1. **Heist briefing** — open on the deck plan again, Marco explains the three rules over the route, then one continuous trip down. Clear, but repeats Part 2's map beat and front-loads talk.
2. **The descent, one rule per level (chosen)** — each rule is a place on the way down: the crew corridor (stay in crew passages), the steel stairs (noise travels through metal), the dark section (light beats speed). Then the engine room and the payoff. The film literally goes down; every scene is darker and closer, and the engines' roar turns the payoff into the cliffhanger.
3. **Start at the engine room, flash back** — open on the lever, then "two hours earlier". Strong hook, but it spends the ending's sound and breaks the series' straight clock.

**Logline:** to steer away from a storm you need the engines, seven decks down. Marco leads you and the cat through the crew-only passages; a dropped wrench is caught an inch from the steel; a torch beam finds an open hatch you'd have run straight into. The engines roar back to life — and every zombie on the ship turns toward the sound.
