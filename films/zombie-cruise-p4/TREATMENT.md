## Candidate structures
1. **Siege on the bridge** — stay on the bridge the whole film while the horde climbs; the rules are told as you defend it. One set, tense, but the "seal the decks" and "lifeboat" beats would be told, not shown.
2. **Up, then out (chosen)** — the horde climbs, you push them back with a fire hose, seal a watertight door behind you, wait on the bridge until binoculars find the coast, then lower the lifeboat together (Part 2's rule paying off) and glide into a dead-quiet harbour. The film moves from the dark inside of the ship to open sea and morning, and the ending turns that relief cold.
3. **Countdown to land** — a recurring distance counter ("12 MILES… 4 MILES…") between scenes. Clear, but it adds a second graphic system next to the series clock.

**Logline:** the whole ship heard the engines, and they're coming up. A fire hose washes a stairway clear, a watertight door slams on reaching hands, and at sunrise Marco's binoculars find land. You lower the lifeboat together and glide into the harbour — where every single light is out. The cat looks back at us.
