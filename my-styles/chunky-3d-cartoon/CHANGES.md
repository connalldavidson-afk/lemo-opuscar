# Chunky 3D Cartoon — decisions (from a blank page; technique base: Brick Toy)

| Section | Base (Brick Toy) | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| §1 Essence | brick world on a real desk, stop-motion | rounded-primitive cartoon world, second-person survival series, calm narrator over chaos | the user's series format and look |
| §2 Materials | glossy ABS bricks with studs, clearcoat, fingerprints | smooth satin rounded primitives, no textures; fog haze; 1× render with MSAA | "chunky 3D cartoon, soft lighting, slightly hazy"; no GPU |
| §2 Light | studio HDRI + softboxes | soft warm key + hemisphere fill + procedural environment + practicals | no HDRI needed; soft cartoon light |
| §3 Colour | brick primaries vs muted real room | bright clothes on people, pastel sets, grey-green zombies, red reserved for header and danger | "bright clothing"; zombies must stay readable and non-gory |
| §4 Type | Fredoka subtitles on a toy pill | fixed header (red banner, cream PART badge, clock pill) + Lilita One 2–3-word captions with shadow, lower third | the user's series template |
| §5 Motion | 12 fps stepping | smooth on ones at 24 fps | cartoon look, not stop-motion |
| §6 Camera | macro toy photography moves | survival-story vocabulary: push-in, top-down, low angle, whip pan, OTS, shake, orbit, hard cut | second-person storytelling |
| §7 Sound | toy instruments, warm storyteller | drone + pulse, stingers, ambience beds, calm matter-of-fact narrator | "calm narrator while chaos happens" |
| §8 Native moves | snap-together building, scale reveal | rule over chaos, clock jump, sidekick, hard-cut cliffhanger | the series format |
| — | separate 15–30 s test film | Part 1 (45–60 s, with voice) doubles as the style's demo film | user approved: one 3D render instead of two |
